Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Overview

Majestic Nickel Tracker is a desktop application designed to help users track nickel mining statistics.

2. Information and Google Account Access

The application uses Google OAuth to allow the user to connect their own Google account and synchronize application statistics with a Google Spreadsheet.

The application requests access to Google Drive files created or selected through the application. It also requests the user's email address to identify the connected Google account.

3. How Information Is Used

Google account access is used only to provide Google Spreadsheet synchronization and identify the connected account.

Majestic Nickel Tracker does not sell, rent, or use Google account information for advertising.

4. Local Data

Application settings, tracking history, and Google authentication information may be stored locally on the user's computer.

5. Data Sharing

The application does not intentionally share users' personal information with third parties.

6. Data Deletion

Users can remove locally stored application data from their computer. Users can also revoke the application's Google account access through their Google Account settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the GitHub profile associated with this project.
